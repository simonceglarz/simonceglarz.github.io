STM32 flight controller

October 2026 to now

About

I started in computer science and transferred to computer engineering in fall 2025. Most of my time outside class goes into the projects above. The balancing robot taught me soldering, KiCad and SolidWorks from zero, and this fall I moved to STM32 and FreeRTOS for the flight controller.

I want to work on embedded systems for aircraft, spacecraft and defense hardware, where firmware has hard timing limits and has to be right the first time. Longer term I’d like to work in Europe. I’m a US and EU citizen, I speak Polish fluently, and I’m learning German.

Outside engineering I keep tropical vivariums, which is where the dart frogs come from, and I follow the Champions League.
